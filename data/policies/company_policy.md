# CloudSuite Inc. — Customer Support & Sales Policy Handbook
## (Internal Reference — v3.2)

CloudSuite is a B2B SaaS project-management platform. This handbook defines
what support and sales representatives are authorized to promise customers.
Any commitment outside these rules requires manager approval before being
communicated.

---

## 1. Refunds & Credits

1.1 Reps may issue a **full refund** for any purchase made within the last
**14 days**, no manager approval needed.

1.2 Refunds for purchases **older than 14 days** require manager approval.
Reps may NOT promise a refund outside the 14-day window without escalating
first.

1.3 Reps may issue **account credit** (not cash refund) up to **$100** per
incident for service disruptions, without approval. Credits above $100
require manager sign-off.

1.4 Reps must NEVER promise a refund for downgrade-related feature loss —
customers were notified of feature changes at time of downgrade per Section 4.

---

## 2. Fee Waivers

2.1 **Late payment fees** may be waived ONE time per account, per 12-month
period, without approval — but only if the account is otherwise in good
standing (no prior fee waivers, no active disputes).

2.2 **Setup/onboarding fees** may only be waived for contracts of
**Enterprise tier or above** ($2,000+/month). Reps may not waive setup fees
for Starter or Growth tier customers under any circumstances.

2.3 Reps may NOT promise to waive fees "as a one-time exception" more than
once per account — this must be tracked and denied on repeat requests.

---

## 3. Pricing & Discounts

3.1 Reps may offer a maximum discount of **10%** off list price on new
annual contracts without approval.

3.2 Discounts above 10% (up to 25% maximum) require manager approval and
must be documented with a business justification.

3.3 Reps may NEVER promise "price lock" guarantees beyond the current
contract term. Future renewal pricing is not guaranteed and must not be
stated as fixed.

3.4 Reps may NOT promise custom/negotiated Enterprise pricing to
Starter or Growth tier prospects without sales engineering + manager
involvement.

---

## 4. Feature Commitments

4.1 Reps may NOT promise specific unreleased features or specific delivery
dates for roadmap items. Only "this is on our public roadmap" may be
communicated, with a link to the roadmap page.

4.2 Reps may NOT promise that a downgrade will preserve access to
higher-tier features. Downgrades immediately restrict feature access per
the current tier's feature table.

4.3 Reps MAY confirm currently-live features and their documented behavior
without restriction.

---

## 5. SLA & Support Commitments

5.1 Standard tier customers are entitled to **48-hour** response time on
support tickets. Reps may NOT promise faster response times to Standard
tier customers.

5.2 Enterprise tier customers are entitled to **4-hour** response time per
their contract. This may be confirmed freely.

5.3 Reps may NOT promise a specific bug fix timeline. Only "this has been
escalated to engineering" may be communicated.

5.4 Uptime SLA is **99.9%** for Enterprise, **99.5%** for Growth, **not
guaranteed** for Starter. Reps may not promise uptime guarantees above a
customer's actual contracted tier.

---

## 6. Contract Terms

6.1 Reps may NOT promise early contract termination without penalty,
except in cases of documented service failure (see escalation policy,
not covered in this handbook).

6.2 Reps may NOT promise that a month-to-month conversion is available for
customers on annual contracts before the contract term ends.

6.3 Auto-renewal terms must be disclosed accurately if asked; reps may not
promise "we'll remind you before it renews" unless the account is
opted into renewal reminders (a real, existing feature).
